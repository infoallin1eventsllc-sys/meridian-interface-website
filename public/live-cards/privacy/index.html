Live Cards privacy

Draft dated 25 November 2026. This page describes the Live Cards Studio and the public card pages.

What we keep

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Your card: the name, title, contact details, address, hours, links, payment names, logo, photo or video you choose to put on it. When you publish, the Studio also makes a small preview picture from your card, so your link shows your name and picture when it is texted or posted.
  • Counts. How many times a card is opened and how many times each button is tapped, so its owner can see it working. We count taps, not people: we do not record who opened a card.
  • Info a visitor chooses to send. If a card has a "Send me your info" button and a visitor fills it in, the name, the phone number or email and any note they typed are kept for that card's owner, who can remove them at any time.
  • Reports that visitors send about a card. A report holds the card's link and what the visitor wrote. It does not record who sent it.
  • Your plan, and if you pay for one, the reference numbers Stripe gives us for your subscription. We never receive your card number.
  • A block list. If an account is blocked for breaking the rules and is then deleted, we keep its email address so the same email cannot start over.

What is public

Everything on a published card is public to anyone with its link. Do not put anything on a card that you want kept private. Your sign-in email is not shown unless you add it to the card yourself.

What stays on your device

Until you publish, your draft, including any photo or video you pick, is stored only on your own phone or computer.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use your photos or videos for anything except showing your card.
  • The Studio and card pages do not show ads.
  • The Studio and card pages do not load outside tracking or analytics scripts, and do not use cookies to follow anyone.

Companies that help run the service

  • Supabase stores accounts, cards and uploaded files, and runs sign-in.
  • Vercel serves the pages. Like any web host, it keeps short-lived technical logs such as the address a request came from.
  • Stripe takes payment for paid plans on its own page.

They handle your information only to provide these services to us.

How we protect it

Each account can read and change only its own card. Nobody can list or search other people's cards; a card opens only for someone who has its exact link. Uploaded files are limited to pictures and video. No system is perfectly safe, so do not put anything on a card that would harm you if it became widely known.

Deleting your information

In the Studio, open Share and choose "Delete my account and card". That removes your account, your card and your uploaded files. You can also take a card offline without deleting it. If you pay for a plan, cancel it first from the Studio so that billing stops.
