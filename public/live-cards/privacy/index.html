Live Cards privacy

Draft dated 24 November 2026. This page describes the Live Cards Studio and the public card pages at meridianinterface.com/c/.

What we keep

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Your card: the name, title, contact details, links, logo, photo or video you choose to put on it.
  • Reports that visitors send about a card.

What is public

Everything on a published card is public to anyone with its link. Do not put anything on a card that you want kept private. Your sign-in email is not shown unless you add it to the card yourself.

What stays on your device

Until you publish, your draft, including any photo or video you pick, is stored only on your own phone or computer.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use your photos or videos for anything except showing your card.
  • The Studio and card pages do not show ads.

Deleting your information

In the Studio, open Share and choose "Delete my account and card". That removes your account, your card and your uploaded files. You can also take a card offline without deleting it.
