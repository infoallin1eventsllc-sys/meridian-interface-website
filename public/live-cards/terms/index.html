Live Cards terms

Draft dated 25 November 2026. By using the Live Cards Studio you agree to these rules.

Your account

  • You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email. Anyone who can read that email can sign in as you, so keep your email account secure.
  • Never share a sign-in code. Meridian Interface will never ask you for one.
  • One account is for one person or one business.

Your content

  • You may only upload photos, videos and logos that are yours or that you have the right to use.
  • You keep ownership of what you upload. You give Meridian Interface permission to store it and show it on your card.
  • Your card must describe you or your own business truthfully. Do not pretend to be someone else.

What is not allowed

  • Content that is illegal, hateful, sexually explicit, threatening or that harasses anyone.
  • Other people's private information.
  • Scams, misleading offers or links to harmful sites.

Reports and removal

Anyone can report a card. We review reports and may take a card offline or block an account that breaks these rules. A blocked account cannot publish or upload, and deleting it and signing up again with the same email does not lift the block. If you think we made a mistake, email us.

Limits

Pictures and videos must be JPG, MP4, MOV or WebM, up to 25 MB each. Videos can be up to 30 seconds. An account can hold a small number of files at a time; older files are removed when you replace them.

Paid plans

  • Making and sharing a card is free. A paid plan, where offered, adds extras.
  • Payment is taken by Stripe on Stripe's own page. Meridian Interface never sees or stores your card number.
  • The price and how often you are billed are shown on that page before you pay.
  • You can manage or cancel a plan from the Studio at any time.

The service

We work to keep cards online but cannot promise the service will never be interrupted. Keep your own copy of anything important.
